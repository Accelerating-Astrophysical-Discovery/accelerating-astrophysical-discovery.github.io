# Day 3: Wednesday - Parallel Data and Model Work

Hours: 9:30-17:00

## Purpose

Wednesday continues the two technical tracks:

- **Data:** relevancy graph, representation, and tokenisation.
- **Model:** architecture, training, and physical alignment.

The morning begins with a shared review of current progress and experimental results, followed immediately by parallel work. The day uses that evidence to select and connect hypotheses for Thursday's implementation.

## Central Deliverable

Reviewed evidence and hypotheses ready to implement.

This should include:

- what exists, what ran, what failed, and what remains untested;
- updated interpretations and retained alternatives;
- specified inputs, outputs, data access, units, checks, and interfaces;
- hypotheses and components selected for Thursday;
- implementation owners, dependencies, risks, and fallbacks.
