# Day 2: Tuesday - Parallel Data and Model Work

Hours: 9:30-17:00

## Purpose

Tuesday uses Monday's scientific workflows to make the open problems concrete. A shared vocabulary and hypotheses kickoff is followed by two parallel technical tracks:

- **Data:** relevancy graph, representation, and tokenisation.
- **Model:** architecture, training, and physical alignment.

Infrastructure considerations support both tracks. The tracks reconnect in plenary and coordinate their interfaces.

## Central Deliverable

First tests of data and model hypotheses and interfaces.

This should include:

- the use case and evaluation target each hypothesis serves;
- concrete linked-data, representation, model, or simulator examples;
- explicit hypotheses, alternatives, and unknowns;
- small inspectable artifacts or tests;
- dependencies and interfaces between the Data and Model tracks.
