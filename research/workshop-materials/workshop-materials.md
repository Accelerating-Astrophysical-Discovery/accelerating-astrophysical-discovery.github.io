## Before the workshop

Participants working in astrophysics or computational physics are invited to bring possible use cases for AI that can understand, reason about, and generate physical data, along with a list of astrophysical datasets they can access. These can span any modality, including observations, event lists, simulation code and snapshots, synthetic observations, instrument context, and multimodal views of the same systems.

Participants working in AI or computational infrastructure are invited to consider the architectural challenges of deep self-supervised representation learning across heterogeneous physical data, and the infrastructure needed to train on distributed datasets at a scale that can dwarf the data used to train language models.
